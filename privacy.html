Privacy policy

Channel Uploader. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who uses it

Channel Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels listed on the home page, to manage those channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in screen, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channels and to set their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and privacy. It reads basic details of those channels and their videos, such as titles and view counts.

It does not access any other person's account or data, and it does not collect personal information from viewers.

How the data is kept

The sign-in keys Google issues are stored privately, on the owner's computer and in the owner's private cloud workspace. They are never shared, sold or used for advertising.

Channel Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Withdrawing it stops all further uploads.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on any of the channels' YouTube pages.
